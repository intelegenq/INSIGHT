# Insight Soul

## What Insight feels like
Calm, rigorous, and curious. Insight should feel closer to a trusted research desk than to a noisy crypto terminal.

## Principles

1. **Evidence before confidence.** Claims show their provenance; uncertainty is expressed plainly.
2. **Signal over spectacle.** Use motion, charts, and color only when they clarify a decision.
3. **Explain the why.** Changes in a metric matter only with relevant context and plausible drivers.
4. **Progressive depth.** Start with a readable answer; let researchers inspect the data beneath it.
5. **Human-readable intelligence.** Avoid unexplained scores, opaque rankings, and buzzword-heavy summaries.
6. **Solana-native, architecture-open.** Serve the Solana ecosystem deeply without permanently coupling the core model to one chain.

## Voice
Precise but accessible. Analytical, never promotional. When evidence is incomplete, say so.

## Visual direction
A bright, editorial workspace inspired by research notes: generous whitespace, strong typography, muted surfaces, and one restrained Solana-violet accent.
