# Insight — Project Master

## Mission
Build the most useful AI research surface for understanding the Solana ecosystem: credible, current, explainable, and pleasant to use.

## MVP outcome
A visitor can open Insight, understand the day’s meaningful Solana activity, explore one project or narrative, and generate a cited research brief without needing to reconcile scattered sources manually.

## Target users
- Ecosystem researchers and analysts
- Builders evaluating adjacent protocols
- Investors and community members seeking context, not price feeds
- Judges evaluating an automated Solana ecosystem report

## Product capabilities
1. **Ecosystem pulse** — an editorial-style overview of important events and metrics.
2. **Project intelligence** — a structured view of a protocol’s traction, context, risks, and evidence.
3. **Narratives** — surfaced themes with trend direction and representative projects.
4. **Research briefs** — shareable, source-backed reports generated from curated signals.

## Non-goals for the MVP
- Trading execution or investment advice
- A generic Solana block explorer
- Uncited model claims
- Exhaustive coverage of every protocol on day one

## Success criteria
- The dashboard is legible within a minute.
- Every material claim can point to a source or a clearly marked synthetic demo datum.
- A generated report is useful as a starting point for real research.
- Data ingestion and AI generation are replaceable interfaces, not UI-only logic.
